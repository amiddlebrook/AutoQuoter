What a custom setup would cover

  1. Discuss the five jobs and the workflow they should cover.
  2. Use prices and labor times provided and verified by the customer, not generated market data.
  3. Check that the estimate arithmetic reflects their intended overhead and markup definitions.
  4. Review manual backup and device limitations.
  5. Agree in writing on scope and price before any checkout link is issued.

What it will not promise

There is no price-feed synchronization, automatic CRM integration, compliance certification, dynamic equipment inventory, payroll processing, or guaranteed pricing accuracy. This is a constrained one-time setup, not a full estimating-system migration.

Is this a paid subscription?

No. The calculator is free and no recurring subscription is currently offered.

Why isn't there a Buy button?

Because the service has not yet established a verified checkout, validated price or fulfillment track record. We won't collect a payment for an undefined deliverable.

Can I use AutoQuoter without paid setup?

Yes. All existing quote calculation and export features remain free.
