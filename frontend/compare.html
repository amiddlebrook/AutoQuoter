Is a free estimating calculator enough?

Many contractors need invoicing, payment collection, scheduling and CRM—not just arithmetic. AutoQuoter does not pretend to replace those systems. Here is where competitors are stronger, and when a local-first estimate worksheet may be preferable.

Choose AutoQuoter if…

You already know your labor/material costs and want a local, low-friction planning worksheet without creating accounts. Pricebook entries and saved quotes remain in the browser until you export them. The tool cannot email customers or collect deposits.

Choose a competing system if…

You need digital signatures, payment collection, mobile team access, synchronized customer accounts, scheduling or invoicing. These are actual buying reasons, and there is no point presenting AutoQuoter as having them.

Try the product before considering paid help.

Use your own inputs first. The optional assisted setup service is an experiment—not a required upgrade or subscription.
