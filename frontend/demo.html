Written quick start

  1. Open Quote studio, choose one of six trades and select a service.
  2. Pick a state, quantity, complexity, scheduling urgency and extra materials allowance.
  3. If you have your own verified numbers, enable Calculate using my rates and costs. Enter all five custom price inputs and optionally save them for that job.
  4. Calculate and check the source disclosure, hours, costs, overhead, markup and subjective range.
  5. Save the quote to this browser, print to a PDF, or copy its summary.
  6. Find saved estimates under Estimates, adjust status and export CSV or JSON backup in Settings. Backup frequently because browser data is not synchronized.
Important: This is an assumption-based planning tool, even when you supply your own rates. It does not certify a legally binding estimate or know permit costs, unknown site conditions or verified live materials prices.
