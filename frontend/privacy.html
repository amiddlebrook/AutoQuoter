Privacy and limitations

Your estimates stay in your browser

This release has no user accounts, subscription tracking or server-side quote history. Browser localStorage holds saved quotes, optional client labels and custom job rates on this device.

What is sent to the API?

When calculating, the app sends the trade, job preset, state, quantity, complexity, urgency, additional materials and any optional pricing numbers to a Cloudflare Worker for arithmetic. The production Worker does not record them in a product database. Project titles and client labels are added after calculation in your browser.

Limits

LocalStorage is not encrypted or synchronized. Shared computers or compromised browser profiles may expose saved information. Clearing site data removes it. Export a JSON backup regularly. Do not enter payment card data, credentials or sensitive regulated information.

Commercial status

There is no direct checkout, automatic payment handling or registration in this version. Anonymous public website requests and platform-level security/telemetry may exist outside the app's own data storage; we do not claim a full external infrastructure data audit.

Optional private feasibility inquiries

This is separate from saved quotes. The optional assisted-setup form stores your submitted email, company label, needs, job list and budget indication in first-party Cloudflare SQLite Durable Object storage for up to 90 days. Your email is unverified; there is no automated email marketing. A daily one-way hash of the connecting IP is used for submission throttling; the raw IP is not stored in the inquiry table. Cloudflare may process request metadata independently.

Only an operator with an administrative secret can list inquiries. There is no guaranteed response, and no automated email notification. Do not share private pricebooks, passwords, payment details or client identities.

Deletion

Use Settings → Clear all estimates or browser site storage for local quotes. For a private inquiry, save the deletion reference provided at submission and use self-service inquiry deletion. Otherwise inquire through a contact route you have already used. Records older than 90 days are cleared during inbox activity, not by an exact timed job.

This is an operational disclosure, not a legal compliance certification.
