# Thirty-day operating sequence — targets, not accomplishments

Day 1: audit code, competitive landscape, privacy, licensing and terms. Decide free utility + service experiment, not subscription.
Days 2–4: complete validated custom pricing, truth-in-advertising labels, browser workflow tests, sitemap, meaningful original calculator content.
Days 5–7: publish narrated actual-UI tutorial using free voice and capture tools; submit sitemap only after account verification. Prove browser saves, print and export.
Days 8–10: research 10 existing published pricing/workflow questions; offer direct technical help only in channels allowing it. Collect 5 buyers' existing solution and pain.
Days 11–14: test 2 individual service messages, collect 10 qualified conversations, gather time estimates and actual monetary objections.
Days 15–21: revise only the largest measured conversion blocker; decide whether free lead magnet yields real setup interest. Don't add D1 login/payments until demand.
Days 22–30: if one pilot accepted, agree scope, verify payment account, charge by approved payment link, deliver, track time/refunds, request authorized feedback. Otherwise recommend pausing AutoQuoter monetization.

**Day 30 scoreboard:** website deploy result, recorded video proof, Search Console indexed pages, number of genuine qualified visitors and conversations, actual proposals, paid orders, gross sales, fees, refunds, net contributions and delivery hours. Unknown data is unknown, not zero.
