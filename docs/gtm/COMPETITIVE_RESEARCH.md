## Demand reality
The existence of paid competitors proves a category of paying customers exists, **not** that consumers will buy AutoQuoter or its setup service. No verified customer interview, sales transaction, domain traffic or retention data for AutoQuoter has been observed.
Free offerings by Contractor+, ServiceM8 and Invoice Ninja mean the proposed $199–$399 manual setup service competes with tools charging $0 in software subscription. Any customer must value actual *configuration labor*, not merely access to a calculator.

## Competitive advantage hypothesis (unvalidated)
- Account-free, browser-local quick planning in six trades, with explicit arithmetic and editable owner-supplied rate/time/materials.
- JSON backup and transparency; no ad tracking or mandatory account for the core tool.
- Best positioned as a free micro-tool and a customer-discovery funnel for optional setup, **not** as a Joist/Jobber replacement.

## Exit criteria
If buyer interviews show no willingness to pay for assisted pricebook setup, maintain the free calculator as a portfolio/demand acquisition asset; prioritize a different repository for direct revenue. Do not create more SaaS features to defend a nonexistent moat.
