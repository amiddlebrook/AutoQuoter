# Zero-dollar acquisition experiment

Positioning: **the transparent, account-free job-price worksheet**, not a new full-service contractor CRM.

Ideal first user: independent estimator or owner with known costs, not someone needing a market-price oracle. Paid-service target: buyer who has at least five recurring job types and specifically wants someone else to implement/test their pricebook.

Top motivations: avoid subscription; understand markup; calculate with verified job costs. Top objections: 'I can use Excel'; 'Contractor+ and ServiceM8 are free'; 'your model is not accurate'; 'browser storage can be lost'; 'no invoicing or customer approval'.

## Channel decision — do not scatter effort
- **Primary:** search-intent utility content (calculator and meaningful explanatory page) — no ad spend; slower traction but scales without spam.
- **Support 1:** genuinely relevant community answers with technical calculations, and a link only when the rules permit it. Reddit is for problem research/contribution, not link drops.
- **Support 2:** explicit one-to-one agency/estimator conversations where a business requests help with pricebooks. No purchased lists, automated mass email, or invented leads.

Deprioritize Product Hunt, broad social posting and big paid listings: likely poor early buyer intent.

## Assets / work
Landing page and utility in PR, transparent comparison in PR, documentation in PR, tutorial to be generated only via real app with offline free tools. Outreach copy: `SALES_PLAYBOOK.md`.

## Experiments and instrumentation
- Search impressions for 3 money-intent queries after Google indexing.
- Manual tracking: 30 qualified permission-based conversations; note baseline tools, whether they have cost data, setup willingness-to-pay, and blockers.
- Success criterion for service: 3 affirmative price-and-scope discussions and 1 actual paid pilot, not 'likes'.
- Failure: no qualified need after substantial outreach; stop feature bloat and reconsider portfolio product choice.

## 0-cost resource constraints
No domains, paid AI, ads, paid analytics, purchased lists, premium recording credits. Cloudflare Worker Free is capped at 100k request/day and 10ms CPU/request; URLs and rate caps subject to account limits.
