# Sales playbook — buyer evidence, not spam

## One-sentence offer
AutoQuoter is a **free**, account-free contractor estimate calculator; optional personalized setup may help a buyer configure five common jobs using their **own** rates, material costs and overhead targets.

## Discovery questions
1. What are the five jobs you estimate most frequently?
2. What tool or spreadsheet do you use and what costs are entered manually?
3. Are your labor hours, rates and material costs recorded and up to date?
4. How long is a typical price calculation? Which mistakes are most costly?
5. What has stopped you from using the existing free or paid competitor tools?
6. If someone set up your pricebook and checked 5 scenarios, what written deliverables would make it worth buying?
7. Would you consider $199–$399 **one-time**, with no mandatory subscription? (Research question, not price commitment.)
8. Who can approve spend, and do they need online invoices/signatures that AutoQuoter lacks?

## Personalized, opt-in first contact
Subject: Quick question about repeated estimate setup

Hello — I saw your published request for help with [specific estimating / pricebook problem]. There are free tools such as Contractor+ and ServiceM8, so I don't want to pitch an unnecessary replacement. I'm testing a simple account-free calculator that lets you use your own labor and material allowances: https://autoquoter.averymiddlebrook2014.workers.dev/pricing-guide.html. If you still need assistance configuring repeated job types, would you be open to a short scoping conversation? There's no obligation or subscription.

Thanks, AutoQuoter

Only contact businesses via legitimate permitted channels; don't send repetitive bulk solicitations.

## Follow-up, only where appropriate
A few days later: “Following up once on the [specific problem]. I can send the free calculation methodology or a sample setup checklist. If this is not useful, I won't follow up again.”

## Objections
- ‘Excel is free’: correct; use Excel if it meets needs. AutoQuoter differentiates with a reusable job preset form and no formulas to maintain.
- ‘Is your pricing data accurate?’: no. Sample values are illustrative. Buyer-provided data controls pricing.
- ‘Can customers approve and pay?’: not currently. Use a complete field service app if that is essential.
- ‘Why pay for setup?’: only if their time savings/value exceeds the written fee; otherwise self-service is free.
- ‘Is this cloud synced?’: no; export backups, avoid shared-device data.

## Quote/terms gate
Written scope for five jobs, customer-supplied materials/labor data, review and correction limits, fees, deliverable definition (JSON/config screenshots or walkthrough), security boundaries, payment/refund terms, timing. No money before both parties agree and merchant processor approval is real.

## Lead evidence table
Capture date, source URL, true buyer intent, fit/priority, permission to contact, approach, reply, call held, actual price accepted, funds received, fulfilled, refund. No false lead generation claims.
